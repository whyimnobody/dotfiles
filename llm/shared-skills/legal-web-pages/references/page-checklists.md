# Legal Web Page Checklists

Use this reference when the request needs a concrete section list, an intake checklist, or a reminder of the product facts that usually control legal copy.

## Core Intake

Collect these before finalizing any draft:

- Legal entity name and product name
- Page type
- Jurisdiction
- Locale and spelling convention
- Product surface: website, web app, mobile app, marketplace, SaaS, e-commerce
- Customer type: consumer, business, both
- Contact channels for legal, privacy, support, abuse, refunds, or DMCA-style notices
- Date fields: effective date, last updated date, notice period if applicable

## Geography and Locale

Resolve these explicitly:

- Governing region: country first, then state/province/canton/county/district if relevant
- Audience region: global, single-country, or region-specific
- Language variant: `en-US`, `en-GB`, `en-AU`, `en-ZA`, `fr-FR`, `fr-CA`, `pt-BR`, and so on
- Local terminology choices: `privacy policy` vs `privacy notice`, `terms of use` vs `terms of service`, `consumer` vs `customer`

If the user wants one global page, draft a general baseline and list the jurisdictions that still need local counsel review.

## Privacy Policy

Usually cover:

- What personal data is collected
- How it is collected
- Why it is used
- Legal bases, if the chosen jurisdiction requires them
- Cookies, analytics, advertising, and tracking technologies
- Sharing categories and processors/service providers
- International transfers
- Retention or deletion approach
- User rights and how to exercise them
- Security language that matches reality
- Children or age-threshold handling
- Contact details and complaint or regulator channels where relevant

## Terms of Use / Terms of Service

Usually cover:

- Eligibility and account rules
- Acceptable use and prohibited conduct
- User content, licences, and moderation
- Intellectual property
- Fees, billing, taxes, auto-renewal, and suspension if applicable
- Beta features and service changes
- Warranties disclaimers and limitation of liability
- Indemnity if appropriate for the product
- Termination
- Governing law, venue, and dispute process
- How the operator may update the terms

## Cookie Policy / Consent Copy

Usually cover:

- Cookie categories and purposes
- Essential vs optional technologies
- Consent and preference-management mechanism
- Third-party tools and advertising partners
- Retention or expiry summary when known
- Browser/device controls and in-product controls

## Subscription, Billing, and Refund Terms

Usually cover:

- Plan structure and trial terms
- Billing cadence and payment methods
- Renewal behaviour
- Cancellation timing and effect
- Refund eligibility and exceptions
- Taxes, credits, and chargeback handling

## Community Rules / Acceptable Use

Usually cover:

- Prohibited behaviour
- Reporting channels
- Enforcement ladder
- Content removal and repeat-offender handling
- Appeal path if one exists

## Drafting Discipline

- Prefer product-specific facts over generic legal boilerplate.
- Use placeholders only for facts the product team must supply.
- Keep obligations and rights symmetrical where possible.
- End with a short list of unresolved items that should be reviewed by counsel or compliance.
